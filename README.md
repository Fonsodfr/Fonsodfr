## Hi, I'm Fonso De Frutos 

Music producer from Tenerife, Spain. I started making tracks in **1995 on FastTracker**,
and three decades later I'm writing the audio tools I always wanted in **C++ and JUCE**.

- 🎚️ **30 years producing** · mixing & mastering engineer since the late 2000s
- 💿 **200+ releases** on digital and vinyl: house, techno, hip hop instrumentals, drum & bass and 90s breakbeat hardcore
- 🎓 Higher diploma in **sound engineering**
- 🏷️ Founder of **Merkaba Sound Records** and its vinyl sub-label **Merkaba Vinyl**
- 🔌 Now building audio plugins under the name **Rave Factory**
